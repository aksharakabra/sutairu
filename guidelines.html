House rules

Sutairu is a small shop. Here is how we keep it kind.

Sharing a look

Share your own photos, or ones you have permission to share. Please don’t upload other people’s pictures, screenshots of other people’s posts, or anything you found online.

Keep it about fashion. Outfits, details, styling ideas. No ads, links to sell things, or spam.

No children. Please don’t share photos of anyone under 18.

Keep private things private. No addresses, phone numbers, school or work names, number plates, or chat screenshots.

Keep it kind. No bullying, hate, or comments about someone’s body or background. No nudity or sexual content.

How sharing works

Looks you share appear on the community shelf straight away. We remove location data from your photos when you upload. If a look breaks these rules, it can be taken down afterwards.

Reporting

See something that doesn’t belong? Press Report on any look. A person reads every report. If a look breaks these rules, we take it down.

Your closets

Your closets and saved looks are private. Only you can see them.

Your public page

Your public page shows your name, the looks you shared, and how many people follow you. It never shows your email or your closets. You can unfollow anyone at any time.

Taking something down

If a photo of you or your work is here and you want it removed, tell us and we will take it down quickly. Write to: [add your contact email here before launch]
